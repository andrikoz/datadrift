# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML/CSS (user's choice). No framework, no build step required; must host on any static host.

## Users

Founders, product leads and small-business owners who need senior engineering help: a mobile app, a frontend application, a data platform, product management, or architecture/reliability work. They arrive evaluating whether this team is credible and senior enough to trust with a build.

## Product Purpose

A company website for a boutique studio of senior specialists. It exists to establish credibility and start conversations (email / book a call). Success: a visitor understands what the studio does, believes it is senior and reliable, and makes contact.

## Positioning

A studio led by an engineer with 15 years across corporate and startup environments, having run engineering departments. Core depth: systems architecture, microservices, scaling, reliability, observability, infrastructure, UX and DX, and now building with AI. Delivery is by a network of associates covering mobile, frontend, data and product management, so the brand is a firm, not a personal name.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Practices: architecture and platform engineering, reliability and observability, mobile apps, frontend apps, data, product management, AI-enabled product building.
- Static site, simple, minimal, elegant.
- Must not be tied to the founder's name; neutral "we" voice.
- Company name: Datadrift. Domain: datadrift.gr. Contact: info@datadrift.gr.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Datadrift. Logo: the 'slipped grid' lockup (3x3 grid of squares whose right column has slipped down, stone centre cell, with the lowercase wordmark), chosen by the user from ten alternatives; source files in brand/. Not tied to a personal name. Tone: simple, minimal, elegant (user's words).

## Evidence on Hand

Confirmed true facts the site may state: 15 years of experience; based in Greece; the team has worked in many startups that scaled up; the lead has run engineering departments in corporate and startup companies; delivery is a small senior core with associates brought in project by project.

No client logos, case studies, portfolio, testimonials or metrics, and the user does not want a portfolio. Future work must not fabricate customers, results or numbers.

## Product Principles

- A firm, not a person: the studio voice carries the experience, not an individual's biography.
- Seniority is shown through precision and restraint, not claims.
- One clear action: start a conversation.
- No invented proof; say what is true and let specificity do the work.
